When does the barrier open?

SituationWhat the program does
Registered vehicle arrivesOnce the plate is confirmed, the system sends an open command to the selected barrier.
Unregistered vehicle arrivesWith "Registered vehicles only" on, the entry barrier stays closed.
Blacklisted plate readThe alarm sounds and the match is logged. With the setting on, the barrier stays closed.
Car park fullYour choice: subscribers only, no entry, or a warning and the barrier opens.
Vehicle standing stillWith the setting on, a vehicle parked in view does not open the barrier.
Second read in the same laneWhile a pass is in progress, a second read creates no new record and no second opening.
GDPR

Number plates are usually personal data; retention and data subject requests are your responsibility

The privacy notice, legal basis and retention period belong to the data controller. PlateVision lets authorised users set retention periods for vehicle images and system logs, and mask identity and number plate data when handling a data subject request. Reading records have no timed automatic deletion and no in-program delete, so their retention and deletion must follow a procedure your organisation sets up separately.

Questions about ANPR

How does ANPR work?

The program reads the plate in the camera image and matches it to a subscriber, visitor or blacklist record. The pass is logged and the barrier follows your settings.

Which cameras does it work with?

IP cameras with RTSP. ONVIF cameras are found by scanning the network; you can also add a camera manually.

How is the barrier connected?

Via Access Panel or a 2-output Ethernet relay board. You test the connection from the settings screen.

How is the parking fee calculated?

Choose a time-based or prepaid tariff. You can set a multiplier per vehicle type and a daily cap.

What happens when the car park is full?

You pick one of three options: subscribers only, no new entries, or a warning and the barrier opens.

What happens when a blacklisted plate is read?

An audible and visual alarm goes off and the match is logged. With the setting on, the barrier stays closed.

Can reports be exported to Excel?

Yes. Readings and reports export as Excel (.xlsx) files. A daily report can also be e-mailed to the recipients you choose.

Is a number plate personal data?

In most ANPR settings, yes: a number plate linked to a person is personal data under GDPR. You can set retention periods for vehicle images and system logs; expired ones are deleted automatically. Reading records have no automatic deletion; on a subscriber's request, identity and plate are anonymised.

Is there a charge for mobile and web access?

No. Mobile and web access is free of charge.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) computer, IP cameras with RTSP and a wired Gigabit network. Hardware is specified in the quotation based on the number of cameras.
