About us

Mika Teknoloji Kontrol Sistemleri develops software for staff attendance and access security, and provides installation, training and support with its own team.

  • 18years of experience
  • 15,000+desktop installations
  • 650+companies in the cloud

What we do

For 18 years we have been developing programs that record staff clock-ins, door access, car park entries and security rounds. We have more than 15,000 desktop installations, and more than 650 companies use our programs in the cloud today.
