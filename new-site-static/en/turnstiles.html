Each event shows the person, card, controller, door, direction and time on one row. The status column shows whether access was granted; the same list can be filtered by person, group, controller or door.

DOOR PERMISSIONS

Who can use which door, on one screen

Grant permissions by group or by door. Select the doors and send them to the controller; each transfer is tracked in the message queue as pending, done or failed.

Controller

Discovered on the network or added by IP address and port.

Reader

Wiegand 26, 32 or 34, exit button, PIN or camera.

Relay and direction

Each reader is given a relay, an open time and an entry or exit direction.

Limits and anti-passback

Anti-passback is set per reader; limits are set per controller.

Unauthorised card presentedThe event is logged and denied attempts are counted on the dashboard.
Unregistered card presentedWith the setting enabled, the card is recorded and can be assigned as a visitor card.
Second entry with the same cardIf anti-passback is enabled on the reader, the event is logged with a PassBack status.
Limit reachedThe controller enforces the limit; the event appears under the over-limit filter.
Access granted manuallyRecorded as a manual entry and shown separately under the manual filter.
Controller statusThe controller list shows status and last-seen time; online and offline data collection are configured separately.

Questions about turnstiles

How does a turnstile access control system work?

The person presents a card, the controller applies the door permissions and the event is logged. Every pass appears in the list with the person, card, door and status.

Which turnstile types are available?

Waist-height and full-height turnstiles, accessible gates and speed gates. The right model for your site is chosen during the quote, based on your traffic and installation location.

Which readers are supported?

The software supports Wiegand 26-, 32- and 34-bit readers, exit buttons, PIN readers, cameras and built-in readers. Compatibility with your existing hardware is confirmed before the quote.

How are card permissions granted?

Permissions are granted by group or by door, then sent to the controller. Each transfer is tracked in the message queue.

What does anti-passback do?

It stops the same card being used twice in a row in the same direction. It is set per reader; a violation is logged with a PassBack status.

How are visitors tracked?

A visitor card is issued, the host and purpose of visit are entered, and controller and door permissions are granted. The report shows entries, exits and the number of people still on site.

Can I see unauthorised access attempts?

Yes. Unauthorised entries and denied attempts are counted on the dashboard and can be found through the status column of the event list.

Can reports be exported to Excel?

Yes. The entry and exit list and the card and permission reports export as Excel (.xlsx) files, and lists can be printed.

Does it work with Time and Attendance?

The program has a setting to transfer terminal data to Time and Attendance (PDKS). The scope is agreed during the quote, based on your installation.

Are access records personal data?

Yes. The privacy notice, lawful basis, retention and deletion are the responsibility of your organisation as the data controller.
