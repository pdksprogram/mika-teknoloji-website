DATA PROTECTION

Clock-ins by card and QR, with no biometric data

Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Board, in Principle Decision No. 2026/921, found that processing biometric data to track working time is not proportionate, and that explicit consent alone does not make it lawful. That is why we install E-SOFT PDKS with card readers and QR codes.

Card reader

Staff present their card to the reader and the record is transferred to the software.

QR scan

Staff scan the QR code at the workplace with the app on their phone.

QR in the app

The app verifies the location and shows a QR code for 30 seconds for the reader to scan.

Location

Location is added to mobile records. A setting can hide it in reports.

On request: fingerprint and face recognition devices

In line with the Board's Principle Decision No. 2026/921, we do not recommend biometric methods for tracking working time. We install E-SOFT PDKS with card readers or QR, and offer fingerprint and face recognition only for purposes other than working-time tracking, once the data controller has separately assessed the purpose, necessity and legal requirements.

For example, at high-security sites where alternatives fall short, access to critical areas can be assessed separately (Board announcement).

Time and attendance FAQ

What is PDKS?

PDKS is the Turkish term for a staff time and attendance system. It records when staff arrive at and leave the workplace using a card reader or QR code, and shifts, overtime, leave and the monthly timesheet are calculated from these records.

Which devices does it work with?

It works with the card and QR readers Mika manufactures; staff cards and key tags are made by Mika too. Staff can also clock in by scanning a QR code with the app on their phone. Fingerprint and face recognition devices can be added on request.

Do fingerprint and face recognition comply with KVKK, Türkiye's data protection law?

Under the Board's Principle Decision No. 2026/921, processing biometric data to track working time is not considered proportionate, and explicit consent alone does not make it lawful. We set up working-time tracking with card readers and QR. If you are considering biometric devices for critical-area security, make that decision with your legal adviser.

How are timesheets calculated?

The calculation runs in three steps: records are transferred from the devices, entries are reviewed, and the monthly timesheet is calculated. Overtime is split out by the rule you choose for the work group; if the weekly 45-hour rule is on, time above 45 hours counts as overtime. Checked months are locked.

Can shifts be planned?

Yes. A shift plan is drawn up per person and per day for the chosen period and sent to an approver, who approves, rejects or asks for changes. Approved plans can be locked. Staff see their shifts in the mobile app.

Can staff clock in with their mobile phone?

Yes. Staff scan the QR code at the workplace with the mobile app, or show the QR code in the app to the reader at the workplace. Location is added to mobile records, and a setting can hide it in reports.

Where do staff submit leave and overtime requests?

Staff submit leave and overtime requests from the mobile app. Requests are approved on the Leave Requests and Overtime Requests screens in the admin panel.

Can reports be exported to Excel?

Yes. Timesheets and reports download as Excel (.xlsx) files.

Can corrections be made after a month is locked?

Locked records cannot be changed. If a correction is needed, an authorised user unlocks the selected staff, the record is edited and the period is locked again.

How do I access the admin panel?

The admin panel opens in a web browser. Staff and managers use the mobile app on their phones.

What should I prepare for a demo?

Just tell us your headcount, shift pattern, number of entrances and any devices you already use. We prepare the demo and quote from that.
